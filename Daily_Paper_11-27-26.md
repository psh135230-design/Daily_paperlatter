- **Efficient editing of mitochondrial DNA using LbCas12a**
  - Authors: Zhong C; Huang Z; Bao B; Yin M; He D; Tian R; He H; Wang Y; Li X; Chen G; Hu Z
  - Journal/Server: Molecular Cell
  - DOI: 10.1016/j.molcel.2026.11.009
  - Summary: mitoCas12-BE3 fuses catalytically impaired LbCas12a with a 43-nt crRNA, an optimized mitochondrial targeting sequence, a cytidine deaminase, UGI and GCN4 dimerization to reach up to 54.84% C-to-T conversion in human and mouse mtDNA, enabling crRNA-array multiplexing, library-scale screening of hundreds of mtDNA sites and editing in living mouse tissue — escaping the scalability limits of TALE-based mitochondrial editors.
  - Tag: ⭐ Core relevance (mitochondrial base editing, editor engineering, extremophile microbiology)

- **Structural basis of RNA-guided DNA integration by type I CAST systems**
  - Authors: Finocchio G; Oberli S; Lampe GD; Schmitz M; Sternberg SH; Jinek M
  - Journal/Server: Nature
  - DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-11196-x
  - Summary: Cryo-EM structures of the type I-F Pseudoalteromonas CRISPR-associated transposase (PseCAST) in a series of functional states, validated by transposition assays, define the stepwise mechanism of transpososome assembly and activation and give a rational-engineering handle on the most active CAST system in mammalian cells.

- **Tailoring a CRISPR-Cas-based epi-editor for decreased cytotoxicity and enzymatic specificity**
  - Authors: Jiao R; Goell J; Li J; Ma AJ; Gong K; Bedford GC; Reed D; Mahata B; Kim S; Shah S; Contreras M; Misra S; Maier KE; Diehl MR; Escobar M; Hilton IB
  - Journal/Server: Cell Chemical Biology
  - DOI: 10.1016/j.chembiol.2026.11.001
  - Summary: Engineering the acyl-CoA binding pocket of human p300 fused to dCas9 yields I1417N, which keeps histone acetylation and gene activation while cutting cytotoxicity and transcriptome/proteome perturbation, plus a crotonylation-biased I1395G variant that rewrites the editor's acylation output.
  - Tag: ⭐ Core relevance (mitochondrial base editing, editor engineering, extremophile microbiology)

- **Structural basis of directionality control in large serine integrases**
  - Authors: Shin H; Pigli Y; Peña Reyes T; Bello AJ; Taiwo-Aiyerin OT; Fuller JR; Olorunniji FJ; Rice PA
  - Journal/Server: Molecular Cell
  - DOI: 10.1016/j.molcel.2026.09.028
  - Summary: Eight SPβ integrase-DNA structures (to 3.15 Å) along the integrative and excisive pathways show how recombination-induced conformational change and RDF-driven repositioning of the coiled-coil subdomain select which DNA sites synapse and lock products against back-reaction — the mechanistic basis for engineering large-payload integration.
